## What I Build

I like the space where product judgment, engineering discipline, and taste meet. My work tends to orbit around:

- Useful software with a clear reason to exist
- Developer tools, automation, and workflow leverage
- AI-assisted systems that reduce busywork instead of adding more of it
- Interfaces that feel fast, intentional, and durable
- Small teams, direct feedback loops, and shipping as a habit

## Current Mode

Building, testing, and refining ideas in public and private. I care about turning rough concepts into working products, then tightening the loop until the product feels obvious.

The work is usually some mix of:

- Designing the smallest useful version
- Writing the code that proves it
- Automating the repetitive parts
- Removing friction until the path forward is clear

## Operating Principles

- **Make it real.** A working prototype beats a perfect theory.
- **Keep the loop tight.** Short feedback cycles reveal the truth faster.
- **Protect the craft.** Speed matters more when the work stays maintainable.
- **Use leverage carefully.** Automation and AI should make the human part stronger.
- **Ship with taste.** The details are part of the product.
